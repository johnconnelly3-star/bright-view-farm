Thoroughbred Breeding & Boarding

Chesterfield, New Jersey

Contact the Farm

Bright View Farm is a full-service Thoroughbred breeding and boarding operation on 270 acres in Chesterfield, New Jersey. Founded in 1975, the farm specializes in the care of mares, foals, weanlings, and yearlings — raising horses for both racing and sales with consistent, unhurried attention.

Seven-time TOBA New Jersey Breeder of the Year — 1986, 2011, 2014, 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025.

Home of Book'em Danno, the 2025 Eclipse Award Champion Male Sprinter.

Winner of the 2025 TOBA National Small Breeder of the Year award.

Both 2025 TOBA honors were shared with co-breeder Gregory Kilka.
